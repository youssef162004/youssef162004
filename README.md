## ⚙️ Automation + Analytics

Automation helps businesses move from slow manual reporting to faster, repeatable, and more reliable analytics workflows.

| Area | How I Use It | Business Value |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Data Collection** | Connect APIs, spreadsheets, forms, and business tools | Less manual copying and fewer missing records |
| **Data Preparation** | Automate cleaning, formatting, and transformation steps | Faster analysis and more consistent datasets |
| **Automated Reporting** | Prepare recurring reports and feed dashboards with updated data | Stakeholders get insights faster |
| **KPI Monitoring** | Track important business metrics and changes over time | Faster reaction to risks and opportunities |
| **Workflow Optimization** | Use tools like n8n to connect data sources, reports, and communication channels | Saves time and reduces operational bottlenecks |

> My goal is to combine **Data Analysis + Business Intelligence + Automation** to help businesses save time, reduce manual errors, and make decisions based on fresh, reliable data.
